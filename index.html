How to Install & Play This Song

Ready to shred to this heavy Hip Hop collaboration? Follow these simple steps to load "Tarantula" into your Clone Hero directory:

  1. Get the Audio: Download or record the audio from the video above. Open it in an audio editor (like Audacity), trim any silence from the absolute start, and save it as an Ogg Vorbis file named exactly song.ogg.
  2. Create a Song Folder: Create a folder inside your Clone Hero Songs directory named Ren - Tarantula (ft. Chris Webby).
  3. Generate files: Copy the two configuration scripts below into separate raw text documents. Save them exactly as song.ini and notes.chart inside your newly created folder.
  4. Scan Songs: Open Clone Hero on your computer, navigate to Settings > General > Scan Songs.
  5. Play: Grab your guitar controller, queue up the track, and hit the highway!
