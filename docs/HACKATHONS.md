## Keep the community project focused

Use the same small learning loop and original/cleared practice exercise. Evaluate new-work windows, existing-repository rules, required APIs, device needs, accessibility, rights, and judging access before committing. Do not change project ethics or imply Ren/Enya endorsement to meet a contest theme. Opportunities are not guaranteed cash, mentoring, or commercial support.

## Maintenance

Search every two weeks for strong music technology, accessibility, education, creative coding, game-development, MIDI/controller, and beginner-friendly builds. Prefer online events and relevant Twin Cities opportunities.

For every candidate verify organizer sources, actual deadlines/timezones, registration cutoff if stated, format/location, eligibility, existing-work rules, required technology, and useful fit. State conflicts rather than inventing eligibility.

Remove expired or cancelled entries from this active list and the landing page. Keep the checked date current. If no suitable events are verified, show that plainly. Do not fill the list with weak matches or expired events.

The landing page hides expired entries using their exact UTC deadline in the browser. Scheduled refresh removes them from source and searches for replacements. Git history preserves prior editions.
